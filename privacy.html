MealGrid Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

  • MealGrid exists to keep your food journal. Your data is used only for that. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your journal is stored in the European Union, and only you can see it. People you invite can see it too.
  • Location and camera details are removed from photos on your iPhone before they're uploaded.
  • You can stop sharing, delete your data, or get a copy of it at any time.

Who is responsible

MealGrid is run by Germán Boggio, who decides how your data is used (the “controller” under the GDPR). For any privacy question or request, write to mealgrid.app@gmail.com.

What MealGrid collects

  • Your account: your email address. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple gives us an identifier for you and an email address, which can be Apple's private relay address.
  • Your name: the name you set in Settings. People you share with see it.
  • Your journal:
    • your meals and the times you set for them
    • when each meal was eaten, or skipped and why
    • notes you write
    • which meals you marked off-plan, and your weekly limit
    Information about what and when you eat can say something about your health, so we treat it as sensitive.
  • Your photos: meal photos you choose to add. They are resized on your iPhone, and their metadata (location, camera and date) is removed before upload.
  • Your time zone: so the people you share with see your meals at your local times.
  • Sharing: who you invited, who accepted, and when a share started or ended.

Before you sign in, everything stays on your iPhone and nothing is sent to us. Meal reminders are scheduled on your iPhone and never involve our servers.

We don't collect your location, contacts, or any data for advertising. We don't use analytics or advertising tools, and we don't track you across apps or websites.

Why we use it

  • To provide the app: back up your journal, sync it between your devices, and show it to the people you choose. This is necessary to provide the service you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Your journal and photos: because they may count as health data, we process them only with your explicit consent (GDPR Art. 9(2)(a)). You give it when you sign in to back up your journal, and you can withdraw it at any time by deleting your data.
  • Sign-in emails: your email address is used to send you sign-in links. We don't send marketing emails.

We don't sell your data and we don't use it to train AI models.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, our hosting provider, in Frankfurt, Germany (European Union). It is encrypted in transit and at rest, and photos are kept in private storage. Argentina recognises the European Union as offering adequate protection.

These services handle data on our behalf, only to run MealGrid:

  • Supabase: database, file storage, sign-in and sign-in emails.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple and app delivery.

Who can see your journal

  • You: on every device where you sign in.
  • People you invite: an invite is a one-time link. Whoever accepts it can see your meals, photos, notes and name. They can't change anything.
  • Stopping: you can stop sharing at any time. Their access ends immediately, and the copy on their phone is removed the next time their app syncs.
  • Others: nobody else can see it. Our database refuses every other request. There's no search or public profile, so nobody can find you in MealGrid.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete your data, it is removed from our servers right away and from backups within 30 days. If you sign out, MealGrid removes your journal from that iPhone. It stays in your account.

Your rights

You can:

  • access your data
  • correct it
  • delete it
  • receive a copy in a portable format
  • object to or restrict how it is used
  • withdraw your consent

Most of this you can do in the app. For the rest, write to mealgrid.app@gmail.com and we'll answer within 30 days. Deleting your account and exporting your data directly from the app are coming in a future version. Until then, email us and we'll do it for you.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. In the EU, that is the one where you live. In Argentina, it is the Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública (AAIP).

Age

MealGrid is meant for people aged 16 and over, and isn't directed at children.

Not medical advice

MealGrid helps you notice your own eating habits. It doesn't give medical or nutritional advice. If you are concerned about your eating, please talk to a health professional.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. If a change affects how your data is used, we'll tell you in the app before it applies.
